## 1. Context & Architectural Responsibilities (Q1, Q8, Q13)

The legacy v4 pipeline combined combinatorial `C(N, 4)` pod search (`CPCV PBO = 0.70` on live-filtered subsets), unadjusted `k=1` unit PnL scoring across drifting `$3k–$385k+` leader accounts, uniform `$200 / M` standalone drawdown choking (`cap_scale`), single-minute liquidity spikes, and closed-episode-only monitoring.

Rather than inventing three independent engines, the redesigned research architecture partitions **responsibilities** across existing owners in `trading-ops` and `copytrade-pm`, reusing [canonical_replay.py](../../studies/canonical_replay.py) and `copytrade_pm.pm03_account` / `pm03_execution` wherever their assumptions fit:

1. **Underwriting & Bench Selection (`pod_constructor` / `study_data`):** Point-in-time (PIT) or explicitly labeled retrospective eligibility screening, uncensored episode underwriting (`ROPN`), structural style labeling as organization tools, and pairwise/portfolio crowding controls.
2. **Portfolio Risk Policy (`portfolio_risk_policy` layer):** Retains per-wallet target contributions before netting, fixes per-seat monitoring references `R_i` at activation, manages dual-clock containment states (`ACTIVE`, `FREEZE_NEW_RISK`, `FLATTEN` / `RETIRE`), and evaluates event-driven seat replacement EV.
3. **Causal Execution & Guardian Simulator (`canonical_replay` + `PM03` where assumptions hold):** Nets eligible wallet targets by symbol, enforces follower-side symbol/gross/liquidity caps and exit priority, applies verified prop-firm rulebooks, and audits ex-post realized 1m participation.
   - **Partial-fill residual policy (research-only):** Any research policy that rechecks or adjusts unfilled residuals is explicitly research-only. After a possible broker dispatch, the live contract requires reconciling actual fills and positions; never silently retry the same one-shot opening or claim equivalence with the signed checkpoint policy ([Q8 evidence](../../reports/2026-11-17__execution-bridge__q8-evidence.md)).

## 2. Point-in-Time (PIT) Universe & Walk-Forward Protocol (Q3, Q9)

- **Evidence-bound PIT discipline:** Per [Q9 PIT evidence](../../reports/2026-11-17__research__pit-universe__q9-evidence.md), `2026-11-04` is the earliest `known_at` receipt timestamp in the examined `wallet_registry.sqlite` snapshot—not proof of earlier non-discovery and not automatic PIT proof afterward. An archived Reservoir fill's execution timestamp (`ts`) is **event time**, not `first_known` time, and the 269-wallet Reservoir cohort is retrospective (`historical_first_known=false`).
- **Universe reconstruction:** At each cutoff `T`, reconstruct eligibility solely from historical identity, cohort, and exclusion receipts known by `T` without applying post-`T` exclusions backward or assuming earlier fills imply earlier discovery; record missing provenance as `UNKNOWN`.
- **Walk-forward structure:** Compare methods on matched rolling splits using one predeclared training window (`60d` or `90d`) followed by non-overlapping `30d` evaluation windows, purging/censoring episodes crossing the training boundary and rerunning Static-v4 per fold rather than projecting Nov 16 frozen candidates backward.
- **Exploratory label:** Pre-existing May–Nov 2026 folds remain **retrospective exploratory**, not fresh holdouts. An unresolved challenge at the end of a `30d` window is **right-censored** unless the firm's contract imposes a hard deadline. Fresh prospective CWSA shadow/paper evidence is required before any live promotion.

## 3. Episode Underwriting, Discipline & Structural Sleeves (Q2, Q3, Q10)

- **Episode definition & `ROPN`:** Define an episode per `(wallet, symbol, direction)` from flat/flip opening through close-to-flat or opposite reversal, attributing entry, exit, and reversal costs consistently. Using only **uncensored** episodes closed and known by the training cutoff, score:
  $$\text{ROPN}_j = \frac{\text{modeled\_net\_pnl}_j}{\text{episode\_peak\_abs\_notional}_j}$$
  Fit Empirical-Bayes partial pooling strictly on training candidates and report episode counts, uncertainty, boundary-censored counts, and still-open exposure separately ([Q10 evidence](../../reports/2026-11-17__research__episode-underwriting__q10-evidence.md)).
- **Separate discipline checks:**
  1. **Post-loss tilt:** Compare peak/entry sizes of newly opened episodes strictly *after* a known closed loss (`known_bar <= start_bar`) against entries after a known closed win. Never include episodes already open when a loss closed. Use `1.50x` as a **provisional** research threshold; insufficient win/loss comparisons (or missing close-receipt evidence) are **`UNKNOWN`**, never a pass or `1.0`.
  2. **Size dispersion & copyability:** Measure completed-episode peak size variation and within-episode scaling separately (`>= 6` uncensored episodes is a minimal floor, not reliable `p95` proof). Judge copyability by replaying the leader's actual size variations under `FIXED_K_NATIVE_QUANTITY`, follower caps, and portfolio barriers (never winsorizing adverse trades away).
- **Structural sleeves & crowding:** Use deterministic uncensored holding-time labels (`intraday_tactical <= 288` 5m bars, `multi_day_swing <= 2016`, `positional > 2016`, plus `mixed` / `unlabeled`) as candidate-organization tools without forcing every sleeve to occupy a seat; treat chop resilience as a portfolio role rather than a per-wallet gate. Control crowding at both pair and portfolio levels, explicitly marking low-overlap pairs (`< 5` concurrent active days) as `UNKNOWN` rather than diversified, and retain UPGMA and unclustered risk-screened baselines in matched evaluation.

## 4. Sizing Frontier, Verified Account Barriers & Conditional EV (Q4, Q5, Q11)

- **Verified CFT account barriers (Correction #1):** On a `$10,000` Crypto Fund Trader (CFT) evaluation account with a `$1,000` static-loss allowance (`10%`), the equity floor is **`$9,000`** (`initial_equity - max_static_dd`), not `$1,000`. Daily loss is checked on mark-to-market equity against the **`00:05 UTC` realized balance** (`initial_balance + realized_pnl - costs + funding`, excluding open unrealized PnL), applying the account's verified fixed or percentage-of-balance daily allowance (`daily_loss_basis="balance"`, optional `daily_loss_rate`) rather than hard-coding `$150/$250/$325` ([Q11 evidence](../../reports/2026-11-17__research__risk-speed-frontier__q11-evidence.md)).
- **Pre-registered 4-tier sizing ladder (hypotheses, not loss limits):**
  - **Tier 1 (Baseline):** Existing `$200 / M` standalone-wallet budget cap unchanged (no silent modifications).
  - **Tier 2:** Training peak-to-trough DD ceiling `$300`; training daily loss ceiling `30%` of the applicable balance-reset daily limit (`$150` at a `$10,000` reset balance).
  - **Tier 3:** Training peak-to-trough DD ceiling `$500`; training daily loss ceiling `50%` (`$250` at `$10,000`).
  - **Tier 4:** Training peak-to-trough DD ceiling `$650`; training daily loss ceiling `65%` (`$325` at `$10,000`).
  Fit tier ceilings on continuous training paths (without the old uniform `cap_scale` choke on Tiers 2–4); evaluate frozen portfolios with the balance-reset Guardian and first-barrier lifecycle. Keep `peak_to_trough_dd` and `static_floor_dd` (`initial_equity - min_equity`) strictly distinct in all sizing and reporting APIs.
- **Joint exposure caps (Tiers 2–4, non-silent bounds):** Enforce jointly—never by post-hoc clipping individual `k_i` or silently relaxing infeasible share bounds:
  - Seat risk-proxy share `<= 45%` for `>= 3` eligible seats (standalone-CDaR proxy, not true marginal risk).
  - Per-symbol net exposure `<= 1.5x` current equity (`$15,000` at `$10k`).
  - Total gross exposure `<= 3.0x` current equity (`$30,000` at `$10k`) after only venue-permitted netting.
- **Three-layer follower liquidity controls (Q5):** (1) Causal `allowed_markets` screen on completed trailing volume/depth; (2) Causal 1m participation entry/increase budget using strictly prior completed volume (missing/stale liquidity blocks increases; no future-bar volume lookahead); (3) Exit priority (split reversals into close-old then open-new, modeling partial/slippage exit risk) + ex-post realized 1m participation audit (`max`, `p95`, `>5%` count, unfilled/delay impact).
- **Conditional Challenge EV:** Evaluate over a `90d` horizon (`60d` sensitivity), reporting pass, static/daily failure, still-open counts, time-to-pass, and turnover. Monetary net cash EV (including both phases, reset/attempt fees, and funded payout assumptions) is reported **strictly conditional** on verified economics; where fees/payouts or failure calibration are unsupported, report break-even assumptions and the risk/speed frontier rather than declaring an EV winner (`10%` fail ceiling is a sensitivity constraint).

## 5. Dual-Clock Containment, Freeze/Release & Bridge Contract (Q6, Q7, Q8, Q12)

- **Fixed activation reference `R_i`:** `$200 / M` (Tier 1) or `s_i * D_tier` (Tiers 2–4), serving as a monitoring reference rather than a loss guarantee. Unknown/non-positive `R_i` is not a pass ([Q12 evidence](../../reports/2026-11-17__research__lifecycle-thresholds__q12-evidence.md)).
- **Provisional research triggers (all uncalibrated hypotheses):**
  - *Alert only:* Episode-time CUSUM (`update_roster_episode_cusum` on unit-copy shadow stream) `s_decay > 2.5` or `s_tilt > 2.5`.
  - *`FREEZE_NEW_RISK`:* Follower attributed open unrealized loss `>= 1.0 * R_i`; OR underwater attributed position duration `>= max(24h, 2 * train_win_hold_p95)` (requires `>= 20` uncensored winning training episodes, else `UNKNOWN`); OR missing/stale data (`>600s` source checkpoint, `>300s` mark/account; escalate after `10m`). Cancels pending entries, blocks new entries/increases/opening side of reversals, lets existing legs follow reductions/closes without lowering `k`.
  - *`FLATTEN / RETIRE`:* Cumulative attributed PnL drawdown (realized + unrealized - fees - funding, persistent activation high-water) `>= 1.5 * R_i`; OR CUSUM kill (`> 4.0` or closed episode `MAE > 2.5 * mae_p95`).
  - *Source withdrawal check:* `AV < $1,000` only on a verified complete observation `<= 600s` old under the separately authorized withdrawal workflow; missing optional `source_nav` is **`UNKNOWN`**, never a withdrawal or auto-flatten.
- **Invariants & release ablations:** Never invent marks or flatten from unknown positions; always re-net and re-check aggregate account limits after any containment change (since dropping one wallet's hedge can raise net exposure); preserve partial fills/residuals and persist freeze/retirement latches across restarts. Compare 3 lifecycle arms: (a) episode-only baseline, (b) flat + 1 clean unit-copy shadow episode (`CUSUM <= 2.5`, no MAE kill, no reopen of stale trades), and (c) next-flat-only release (data-only freezes clear after 2 consecutive fresh complete checkpoints + reconciliation). On retirement or prolonged freeze, compare leaving risk unused vs selectively resizing healthy seats vs admitting the top PIT bench seat under strict `new-position-only` after reconciling exposure.
- **Execution bridge boundary:** In `prop-production`, `portfolio_release_v1` enforces `0 < k <= 10`, and `copy_mandates.py` (`copy_mandate_v1`) supports `PAUSE` / `RETIRE` with **no** same-generation reactivation edge ([test_copy_mandates.py](../../../prop-production/tests/test_copy_mandates.py#L122)). Research unfreeze is **never** a live permit; live reactivation requires a new approved mandate generation via `Contain -> Replace -> Version` and `Bybit` order/fill reconciliation (`Cancelled` orders can carry executed qty; `positionIdx` must be verified). Research `new` (flat-to-open/flip) and `copy_mandates` Q15 (guarded late entry on genuine increase) are distinct policies.
