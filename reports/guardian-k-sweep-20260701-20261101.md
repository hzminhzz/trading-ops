A 2× multiplier targets $166.67 per wallet on the calibration window. Static equity failure means **equity <= $9,500**, **not** a portfolio peak-to-trough DD over $500.

## Risk/Guardian scenario assumptions

The new `studies/guardian_k_sensitivity.py` derives one-minute, k=1 account PnL and contemporaneous **gross notional** from the existing `copytrade_pm.pm03_account.Account` and `pm03_execution.CostModel` follower replay, and scales by each training-frozen wallet k. Source targeting remains `FIXED_K_NATIVE_QUANTITY`, reconstructed at exact UTC 5-minute checkpoints from the documented retrospective wallet fills + historical position snapshots. Price path uses local Hyperliquid 1m with connected/cached Vike and available local normalized Reservoir fallback. The market gap policy remains pair-local; no global shutdown on one missing market.

**The tested 24h Guardian is a hypothetical research overlay**, not the deployed `prop-production.Guardian` or an order-preserving native PM03/PM04 portfolio journal:
- Hard contract: $300 UTC-day-start **equity** drawdown, static equity floor $9,500; we separately track drawdown from intraday day-peak as a stricter diagnostic. The historical provider-specific equity/cash daily-basis is **not independently verified**.
- Internal stop default: when equity is $250 below either start-of-day or that day's high, or equity <= $9,600.
- Observe on minute-close; flatten **next minute**, debit an adverse **60 bps of modeled gross** for the whole portfolio; fully flat **24 hours**; hypothetical re-entry on next 5-minute UTC boundary, debit **8 bps** gross entry cost. Reentry rebases to contemporaneous leader target, ignoring all PnL during the hold. Risk breach while liquidating is permanent.
- Stress A: $220 internal daily, **5-minute** flatten, **120 bps exit**, **16 bps reentry**.
- Stress B: $200 internal daily, **10-minute** flatten, **200 bps exit**, **20 bps reentry**.
- Manual-latched proxy: same as default, but **never** auto-restart (more faithful to current protected HALT requiring signed remediation/explicit arming).

These are deliberately distinct *policy assumptions*, not actual broker order receipts. Sizing and trading/cost decisions during the 24h pause are approximated from precomputed unit-PM03 curves, not fully replayed source-and-broker ledger sequences. Exit costs use **sum of wallet gross**, conservative relative to possible internal netting but not independent executable quote or capacity validation. The model omits cross-wallet netting, funding, actual margin, lot/minimum order precision, partial fills and all live Guardian authentication.

## Guardian implementation fact

Inspected `prop-production/src/prop_production/journal.py` and `recovery.py`. A protected HALT **does not auto-clear after 24h**. Clearing it requires independently authenticated reconciliation, an authorized signature, protected-journal state transitions and then rearming. The risk owner exposes protective-reduce actions, but this study did not establish a live automatically successful flatten-or-restart path. A future autonomous 24h pause should require a separately verified design that **never** weakens protected recovery, storage, unknown-outcome or contractual-breach latches.

## Verdict and next research sizing

**EXPERIMENT 1.75×–2× original wallet k, research/shadow only.** The 1.75× case raises TriadA hypothetical returns from $742 to ~$1,299 without a halt in the default proxy; 2× raises passive returns to ~$1,484, with stronger sensitivity to hypothetical Guardian fill/recovery. A 1.5× scenario is the lower-risk comparator; **do not treat 2.5×–4× as accepted** just because the biased observed sample produced more simulated PnL.

Nonnegotiable evidence gaps remain: July–October sources are *ex-post* reconstructed using later position snapshots, ACT04 has documented inventory mismatches/corrections, some 5m target instruments lack source marks, portfolios were selected after the historical evaluation period, 1m OHLCV is not a bid/ask/capacity feed, and actual account margin/funding/order economics/Guardian 24h recovery have not been signed or independently tested. Thus **no multiplier or portfolio is scientifically qualified or trading authorized**. Observe prospective native first-known journals and broker-verified flatten/reentry before selecting a production multiplier.
