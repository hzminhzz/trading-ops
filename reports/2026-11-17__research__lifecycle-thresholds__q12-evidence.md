## Existing behavior and contract gaps

- The [episode CUSUM](../studies/pod_constructor.py#L2012) uses slack `0.5`, warns on either score **strictly greater than** `2.5`, and emits its advisory kill when either score is **strictly greater than** `4.0` or episode MAE is **strictly greater than** `2.5 × mae_p95`. It operates on episode inputs with a matching baseline, not the Q10 ROPN score, and does not submit orders.
- The helper's `KILL_CONTAIN_0X` status is not a durable terminal latch. In a synthetic call sequence with baseline mean `0`, standard deviation `1`, and episode returns `−5` then `+5`, it returned `KILL_CONTAIN_0X` at `s_decay=4.5`, then `OK` at `s_decay=0.0`. The research policy must explicitly latch and persist retirement if that is the desired contract; this observation does not describe a live service.
- The inspected [checkpoint mandate transitions](../../prop-production/src/prop_production/copy_mandates.py#L192) contain pause/retire, but no paused-to-active transition. The corresponding [test](../../prop-production/tests/test_copy_mandates.py#L122) rejects same-generation reactivation and requires a new generation. Signed pause preserves obligations; it does not itself flatten or prove the proposed leader-following freeze is live.
- The [protected checkpoint ADR](../../prop-production/docs/ADR-CANONICAL-5M-CHECKPOINT-GRILL-20261111.md#L53) requires measured source freshness and distinguishes missing from verified flat. The legacy executor's [`600000` ms default](../../prop-production/src/prop_production/bybit_executor.py#L184) is an observed code default, not proof of the installed policy or a calibrated source limit. Source NAV may be null for the default fixed-k policy ([production sizing contract](../../prop-production/docs/SIZING-POLICY.md#L12)); an absent account-value observation is not proof of withdrawal.

## Proposed preregistration

1. Define `R_i` at activation and keep it fixed: for the v4 baseline, `$200 / M`; for the three larger Q11 tiers, `R_i = s_i × D_t`, where `s_i` is that member's frozen standalone-CDaR allocation proxy and `D_t` is the tier's `$300`, `$500` or `$650` training peak-to-trough ceiling. This is a reference for a signal, not a realized loss allocation or guarantee. Unknown or nonpositive `R_i` is not a pass.
2. Compute wallet-attributed cumulative realized plus marked unrealized follower PnL `C_i(t)`, including fees and funding once, from reconciled netted positions. Its running high-water is `H_i(t) = max(0, max(C_i(u)))` from activation through `t`; persist it without resetting on freeze. Freeze when `max(0, -sum(open-leg unrealized PnL)) >= 1.0 × R_i`. Independently freeze if a currently underwater attributed follower position has been open `>= max(24h, 2 × training p95 winning hold)`, measured from its first follower fill. Use the same uncensored episode definition, strictly positive net PnL for wins, the nearest-rank percentile and at least 20 winning training episodes; otherwise the duration trigger is `UNKNOWN`. Continue the existing CUSUM on the same causal unit-copy shadow stream in both active and frozen states; update once when a complete episode is known. Its warning is alert-only. Invalid required attribution, fee/funding evidence or CUSUM baseline is `UNKNOWN`, freezes new risk, and is not a flatten by itself. Explicit common historical cost assumptions may be used in research, labeled as modeled rather than actual. Do not mix CUSUM baseline units with ROPN or actual broker PnL. Account-level Guardian remains independent. CUSUM alarm properties depend on process assumptions ([NIST CUSUM](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section3/pmc3231.htm)).
3. Trigger flatten/retirement at attributed cumulative PnL drawdown from the activation high-water `>= 1.5 × R_i`, or the existing episode-CUSUM kill. A complete source account-value observation, received and verified by the decision time and observed no more than 600 seconds ago, triggers the separate `$1,000` rule if below that amount; it is not proof of withdrawal. Missing optional NAV alone does not trigger flatten or a data-health freeze, but its point-in-time coverage is `UNKNOWN` and this trigger cannot be claimed validated. Any live action remains under the authorized withdrawal/Guardian workflow.
4. Preserve a separate data-health freeze: missing/incomplete required source checkpoints, marks or broker data freeze new risk immediately. For this experiment use 600 seconds for the latest complete source checkpoint and 300 seconds for a mark/account snapshot; escalate a continuous data-health freeze after 10 minutes. These are uncalibrated research settings. Never invent a mark or assume liquidation when exposure is uncertain; maintain independently authorized protective actions. Require two consecutive fresh complete checkpoints plus broker/position/order reconciliation before removing a data-only freeze.
5. At a risk freeze, persist the identities of all currently open attributed episodes; add any newly implicated open episodes while frozen. Block new entries and increases, cancel pending entries, and allow otherwise-authorized reductions or closes. Persist causes, CUSUM, activation high-water and pending effects across restart. Once all recorded episodes are actually flat, orders are reconciled and no data-health freeze remains, require **one fully observed unit-copy shadow episode** begun and closed after flat, with both CUSUM scores at or below `2.5` and no MAE kill. Shadow observations are hypothetical, not broker fills. After release, admit only new source openings or genuine flips; never reopen a stale position. If the shadow episode never closes or has gaps, the risk freeze remains. A data-only freeze needs no clean episode, but an unresolved source gap still prevents stale new entries.
6. Once flatten/retirement is triggered, latch the mandate generation as non-opening through restart, reconcile partial fills and residuals, and perform Q7's explicit replacement/EV evaluation. In the checked signed checkpoint contract, reactivation requires a new approved mandate generation; the research unfreeze is not a same-generation live permit.
7. Pre-register a matched episode-only baseline, the proposed rule, and a next-flat-only release ablation. Use causal observations and the same portfolios, sizing, costs, execution and account rules. Report 90-day modeled EV, pass/failure/unresolved, time-to-pass, missed profitable opportunities, prevented losses, freeze duration, turnover and partial fills. ## External execution constraint

[Bybit's order documentation](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/order/create-order) says a market order is converted to an IOC limit order and may not execute or may be cancelled when liquidity is insufficient. An acknowledged flatten is therefore not proof that exposure is gone. Account-level netting can make removal of one wallet's hedge increase the broker's net exposure; re-evaluate the aggregate book and obey independent Guardian limits. A wallet-level reduction is not necessarily a venue `reduceOnly` order.
