### Instrument-local fallback policy

After the above data sources were checked, each follower instrument is marked independently. **Short quiet windows are not classified as a data outage.** When a follower instrument has no observed traded close for **more than 30 minutes**, the modeled position is **hypothetically closed** at the last real observed close with **50 bps adverse-price stress plus a separate 4.5 bps fee**. That pair is disabled for the rest of the replay; other pairs and wallets continue. This is a deliberately documented **counterfactual risk sensitivity**, not proof of a market-order fill at a stale quote. The event records source mark time, staleness, position, modeled exit price, fee, and `executed=false`.

The final July–October replay needed **zero hypothetical forced closes** after Vike retrieval and use of the 30-minute trade-gap tolerance. Regression tests exercise the actual forced-close branch on synthetic missing-price data and verify the other instrument keeps trading. A stress threshold is not an exchange liquidity guarantee.

## Provenance / significance limits

- Historical native CWSA first-known receipts do not cover July–October. Historical source positions are reconstructed from fills **backward from future full-book snapshots**. This is explicitly **not** an authentic first-known or valid independently prospective replay.
- ACT04 source inventory had three disagreements in 133 sampled snapshots (max 12.49 token quantity) and 239 historical snapshot-override corrections. These are material **data-quality flags**.
- ACT04 recorded 3,082 checkpoints with one or more missing source marks, totalling 3,438 attempted missing-symbol quotes. Those changes were not invented as filled orders. Even though the equity path advances across the entire date range, no **complete-candidate economic qualification** can be inferred. The other two portfolios share ACT04 or have their own uncertainties.
- Some source leader instruments lack quotes at certain attempted checkpoints. Their source *intent* is skipped for those instruments, not silently interpreted as a flat wallet; `pending_unpriced` retries. This may bias portfolio economics even if no existing follower leg was forced-closed.
- Price-based modeled normal orders use the previously completed 1m close plus native PM03 `CostModel` (4.5 bps fee, 2 bps full spread, 2 bps slippage) and native `target_from_fixed_k` over signed leader position quantities, not raw leader fill mirroring. No real L2/market lot precision/capacity, funding cashflow, native first-known clocks or broker fills are certified.
- This simulation **did not deploy** to `copytrade-pm`, `prop-production`, `trading-connector`, Guardian or live trading. `accepted_executable_k=null`, `TRADING_AUTHORIZED=false`.
