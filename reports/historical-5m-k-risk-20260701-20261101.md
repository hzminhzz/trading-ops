# Historical five-minute risk-budget `k` correction — July 1 to November 1, 2026

**Classification:** `RESEARCH_PROPOSAL / RETROSPECTIVE_SCENARIO_ONLY / NO_TRADE`
**Implementation:** `trading-ops/studies/backtest.py` → `historical_5m_wallet_risk.py`, Python 3.14, `uv run --frozen`.
**User-specified challenge:** $10,000; $300 maximum daily equity drawdown; $500 maximum **static** total loss (equity floor $9,500, not trailing).

## Corrected defaults and sizing

- Window: **[2026-07-01 00:00 UTC, 2026-11-01 00:00 UTC)**, July through October inclusive.
- Calibration: **[July 1, October 1)** (July–September); chronological October **not** used to choose `k`.
- **Risk allocation assumption:** three source wallets share the **$500 static account loss budget equally**, $166.6667 per wallet. Allocate only **50%** of each budget to its modeled peak-to-trough wallet loss: **$83.3333** per wallet. This assumption is configurable in the pure function and requires owner review if unequal risk sleeves are desired.
- Correct wallet sizing, without the earlier arbitrary `0.00002..0.001` grid:
  `k_w = min(10, 0.5 * ($500/3) / train_unit_wallet_maxdd_at_k_1)`, rounded **down** to 12 decimal places. This is not optimized using October or November outcomes.
- **K scales signed leader position notional**, not fill quantity: native `copytrade_pm.pm_exposure_sizing.target_from_fixed_k` computes `source_qty * source_price * k / follower_price`. The unit-risk sensitivity uses native `pm03_account.Account.simulate_fill/mark` and `pm03_execution.CostModel.price_and_fee` on local Hyperliquid 1-minute bars.

## Input provenance and scientific blockers

The genuine native **CWSA source receipt journal starts November 12, 2026**. It does **not** contain actual first-known source books, publication clocks, gap/recovery transitions for July–October. Thus the July–October result can only use an explicitly **retrospective five-minute position reconstruction**:

1. Resolves wallet identities and exclusions from the read-only canonical SQLite registry.
2. Reads only `copytrade_pm.wallet_store.scan("fills"/"positions")` for the requested wallet and date range. Selected main-Dex perp scope, spot and builder Dex **not silently included**.
3. Uses future historical position snapshots as an **ex-post anchor**, reverse-applies prior signed fills to derive July 1 initial inventory, then updates at 5-minute UTC checkpoints. An archived snapshot can override an inconsistent fill-derived book, with corrections counted. It is **not** evidence that the follower could have known that book in July.
4. Uses only *local* Hyperliquid 1-minute OHLCV (Polars from `ml4t-data/.data/store`). At execution, uses last **completed** minute's mark, 4.5bps fee + 2bps full spread + 2bps slippage. For deriving the *linear unit-k sensitivity*, trade volume caps and actual exchange lot constraints are deliberately disabled; results cannot claim feasible execution.
5. Marks open follower positions every available minute in the native PM03 in-memory account ledger. Stops, rather than fabricating flat positions or price, at an unmarkable open position.

Important failures retained:
- **ACT04**: 133 archived checkpoint samples audited; **three** sample discrepancies around September 17 (max 12.49 token units), and **239** retrospective inventory corrections during full 1-minute source reconstruction. These are not original CWSA continuity proofs.
- **ACT09**: **68** attempted source-book changes lacked a full local price vector; skipped, not filled. This affects its modeled economics.
- **Source backcast:** ACT04 first independently available archived full position snapshot August 1; ACT08/ACT09/W031 first complete snapshots September 29. The July source books were **backsolved using future snapshots**. Therefore the apparent July–September `k` calibration is not a genuine point-in-time training experiment.
- **No full October coverage:** AZTEC/GMX/SEI local bar gaps prevent a full October evaluation.
- **Funding absent:** local wallet store has no complete funding partitions; no actual funding/borrow, liquidation, displayable min-order/lot, market impact, or broker fills are certified.
- **No actual portfolio netting or protected risk authority:** combined results are a mathematical sum of unit pod PnLs, not a signed PM03/PM04 follower journal and not Guardian authorization.

The script can produce *numerical hypothetical k* from the specified budget, but no wallet or portfolio obtains an `accepted_executable_k`; each remains `null`. The next valid scientific upgrade requires historical full books with genuine contemporaneous first-known receipts and complete 1-minute market marks, plus independent netted execution/risk replay.
